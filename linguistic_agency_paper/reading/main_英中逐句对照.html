LINGUAFORCE 英中逐句对照

Language can push a listener toward an action, narrow the available choices, or do both. This paper presents LINGUAFORCE, a benchmark for measuring this negative agentive force in de-identified multi-turn dialogues. It contains 4,066 dialogues: a 3,432-dialogue training split and a disjoint 634-dialogue held-out split. Each dialogue is represented with a taxonomy of four families and fifteen types, seven measurement dimensions, and linguistic cue guidelines. The benchmark evaluates label consistency, agreement between two independent annotators, three prediction tasks, a two-stage dimension-aware pipeline, and transfer to MentalManip, MultiManip, TalkDown, and ToxicChat.

语言可以推动听者采取某种行动、缩小其可选范围，或者同时产生这两种作用。本文提出 LINGUAFORCE，一个用于衡量去标识化多轮对话中负面话语能动力的基准。数据集共包含4,066条对话，其中3,432条构成训练划分，634条构成互斥的留出划分。每段对话都使用四大类、十五种类型的策略分类体系、七个测量维度以及语言线索说明进行表示。本文评估标签一致性、两名独立标注者之间的一致性、三个预测任务、两阶段维度感知流程，以及向 MentalManip、MultiManip、TalkDown 和 ToxicChat 的迁移能力。

Agentive force, computational social science, psycholinguistic framework, large language models, manipulation detection.

能动力、计算社会科学、心理语言学框架、大语言模型、操纵检测。

Introduction
引言

Language can change what a listener sees as possible or acceptable. A request, threat, moral appeal, or deceptive statement can make one action easier to choose and other actions harder to choose. This listener-directed effect is called agentive force of discourse. The benchmark focuses on its negative forms: language that pressures, constrains, or steers a listener against a neutral choice. Ordinary requests, advice, and rational persuasion are included as controls.

语言可以改变听者认为哪些行动是可能的、可接受的。请求、威胁、道德诉求或欺骗性陈述，可能让某个行动更容易被选择，也让其他行动更难被选择。本文把这种面向听者的作用称为话语能动力。本文关注它的负面形式，也就是让听者承受压力、受到约束，或偏离中性选择的语言。普通请求、建议和理性说服作为对照纳入数据集。

Existing NLP resources cover parts of this problem. Toxicity and condescension datasets focus on explicit hostility [toxicchat2023, talkdown2023]; persuasion datasets often target news text and rhetorical framing [piskorski2023]; and dialogue datasets on psychological manipulation focus on narrower covert strategies such as gaslighting and intimidation [mentalmanip,multimanip,reament]. Compliance-gaining research offers useful strategy taxonomies [marwell1967,wiseman1981, cialdini1984], but these resources use different label spaces. The central question is whether different negative language strategies can be represented and measured in one space. Transfer to strategies outside the annotation is a downstream test of that representation.

现有 NLP 资源覆盖了这个问题的部分方面。有毒语言和居高临下表达数据集关注明显的敌意 [toxicchat2023, talkdown2023]。说服技巧数据集通常研究新闻文本和修辞框架 [piskorski2023]。心理操纵对话数据集则关注煤气灯操纵、威吓等较窄的隐蔽策略 [mentalmanip,multimanip,reament]。顺从获取研究提供了有用的策略分类体系 [marwell1967,wiseman1981, cialdini1984]，但这些分类体系没有提供一个统一的 NLP 测量空间，因而不同样本的强度和维度分数难以比较。本文的核心问题是：不同的负面语言策略能否用同一个空间表示和衡量。对标注范围之外策略的检测，则作为该表示的下游检验。

To address this gap, LINGUAFORCE defines a shared representation and tests whether it is useful for prediction and transfer. Its contributions are:

为解决这一问题，LINGUAFORCE 建立统一表示，并检验它是否有助于预测和迁移。本文的贡献如下：

A unified three-layer framework. The framework combines a four-family, fifteen-type strategy taxonomy with seven measurement dimensions and cue guidelines. The dimensions separate the force of a push, its effect on the listener's options, its pressure channel, and its directness.

统一的三层框架。该框架将四大类、十五种类型的策略分类体系与七个测量维度及语言线索说明结合起来。这些维度分别描述推动行动的力度、对听者选择空间的影响、施压渠道以及表达的直接程度。

A new annotated benchmark. The release contains 4,066 dialogues with dialogue-level intensity and seven-dimension annotations, organized into a training split and a disjoint held-out split. The benchmark relates dimension scores to binary pressure and intensity labels.

新的标注基准。发布版本包含4,066条对话，提供对话级强度和七维标注，并分为训练划分和互斥的留出划分。基准分析维度分数与压力二分类、强度标签之间的关系。

An experimental protocol. The benchmark defines detection, strategy recognition, and intensity prediction tasks. It also provides a two-stage dimension-aware pipeline, a direct zero-shot baseline, and tests of dimension structure and transfer to unseen types.

实验方案。基准定义检测、策略识别和强度预测三个任务。同时提供两阶段维度感知流程、直接零样本基线，并检验维度结构以及向未见操纵类型的迁移能力。

Related Work
相关工作

Datasets of Manipulation and Persuasion
操纵与说服数据集

Compliance-gaining research provides the conceptual starting point. Marwell and Schmitt [marwell1967] and Wiseman and Schenck-Hamlin [wiseman1981] proposed strategy inventories, and Cialdini [cialdini1984] organized common influence principles such as reciprocity, social proof, and authority. In NLP, SemEval-2023 Task 3 detects persuasion techniques in online news [piskorski2023]. MentalManip, MultiManip [mentalmanip,multimanip], and ReaMent [reament] study covert manipulation in dialogue. These resources use different units and label spaces. Many omit ordinary non-coercive controls such as requests, advice, and rational persuasion. Toxicity [toxicchat2023], condescension [talkdown2023], and microaggression datasets [breitfeller2019] cover hostile language, but hostility is only one possible pressure channel.

顺从获取研究为本文提供了概念起点。Marwell 和 Schmitt [marwell1967] 以及 Wiseman 和 Schenck-Hamlin [wiseman1981] 提出了策略清单，Cialdini [cialdini1984] 则归纳了互惠、社会认同和权威等常见影响原则。在 NLP 领域，SemEval-2023 Task 3 检测在线新闻中的说服技巧 [piskorski2023]。MentalManip、MultiManip [mentalmanip,multimanip] 和 ReaMent [reament] 研究对话中的隐蔽操纵。这些资源使用不同的单位和标签空间，许多资源没有纳入普通的非施压对照，例如请求、建议和理性说服。有毒语言 [toxicchat2023]、居高临下表达 [talkdown2023] 和微攻击数据集 [breitfeller2019] 覆盖敌意语言，但敌意只是可能的施压渠道之一。

LINGUAFORCE addresses this gap by putting ordinary non-coercive controls and negative, listener-restricting strategies in one dialogue-level measurement space. It does not replace these resources; it provides a common representation for comparing the pressure they study.

LINGUAFORCE 将普通的非施压对照与限制听者选择的负面策略放进同一个对话级测量空间，以弥补这一缺口。它不替代这些资源，而是提供一种共同表示，用于比较这些资源所研究的压力。

Speech Acts and Pressure
言语行为与能动力

Speech-act theory studies how people use language to perform actions such as requesting, ordering, and promising [austin1962,searle1969]. This perspective helps explain why aggressive words alone cannot identify pressure. For example, “A good daughter would stay” contains no insult, but invokes a family obligation to pressure the listener to stay. The analysis identifies the action requested by the speaker and the ways the dialogue makes refusal harder. The annotation records pressure expressed in the dialogue, not whether the listener actually complies.

言语行为理论区分话语表达的内容与它在互动中执行的行为 [austin1962,searle1969]。我们据此界定标注对象：关注的是话语是否改变听者可采取或可接受的行动，而不是其中是否包含攻击性词语。本文所说的话语能动力也不同于人类学中以说话者为中心的语言能动性概念 [ahearn2001]；这里关注的是听者决策空间所承受的压力。

Normative Influence
规范性影响与道德基础

Normative influence encourages compliance by appealing to duties, social roles, moral expectations, or authority. For example, “You are the team leader; it is your duty” uses a role obligation to pressure the listener. Research on moral foundations provides background for understanding moral and authority-based appeals [graham2009]. These forms of influence motivate the normative-pressure dimension in our framework.

规范性影响借助义务、角色、道德、权威或群体身份发挥作用。道德基础理论 [graham2009] 为组织 C 类策略提供了帮助。例如，C1 运用道德义务，C4 运用权威。两者都不同于威胁，因为它们通过规范或角色来说明所要求行动的合理性。

Framework: A Unified Account of agentive force of discourse
框架：话语能动力的统一描述

Negative agentive force is represented at three levels. The strategy layer describes what the speaker does. The measurement layer records how much pressure is applied. The cue layer links each judgment to words and discourse patterns that can support it.

本节定义什么属于负面能动力，并构建数据集和实验所使用的表示。我们从三个层面表示负面能动力：说话者做了什么、施加了多大压力，以及哪些语言线索支持这一判断。这三个层面构成策略层、测量层和线索说明层。

Definition and Inclusion Criterion
定义与纳入标准

The agentive force of discourse of an utterance or dialogue is the pressure it places on a listener's behavior or decision space. It can push the listener toward the speaker's preferred action or make other actions harder to choose. There are three properties of agentive force. First, the pressure does not need to succeed; it can be present even when the listener resists. Second, one utterance can use more than one channel, such as guilt and social proof. Third, pressure has a degree, so strategies can differ in strength as well as type.

一句话或一段对话的话语能动力，是它对听者行为或决策空间施加的压力。它可以推动听者采取说话者偏好的行动，也可以使其他行动更难被选择。标注时，我们采用三个性质。施压不必成功；即使听者抵抗，压力仍可能存在。一段对话可以同时结合内疚诱导和社会认同等渠道。最后，压力具有程度差异，因此策略不仅类型不同，强度也可能不同。

A discourse has agentive force of discourse when it targets a recognizable listener action or decision and tries to drive or restrict that behavior. The pressure may come from a request, inducement, norm, emotion, deception, or hostility. The taxonomy provides labels for the cases covered in this dataset, but it is not intended to cover every possible strategy. Pure information exchange and emotional venting without a behavioral target are negative examples.

如果一段话语满足以下条件，就认为它具有话语能动力：（i）针对听者某个可识别的行动或决定；（ii）试图通过施压、利诱、援引规范、利用情绪、欺骗或敌意来推动或限制该行为。下文的分类体系为这些机制提供标签。它并不旨在覆盖所有可能的策略。不具有行为目标的纯信息交流和情绪宣泄属于负例。

Three-Layer Architecture
三层架构

Layer 1 describes the speaker's tactic with fifteen types in four families. Layer 2 records the amount and source of pressure along seven dimensions. Layer 3 connects the scores to observable words, constructions, and discourse patterns. For example, an ultimatum can receive a high option-constraint score because it removes alternatives, while an indirect moral appeal can receive a high normative-pressure score even without an insult. The three layers make different strategies comparable and provide evidence for error analysis.

图 [fig:framework] 展示了该架构。第一层通过四大类、十五种类型描述说话者在做什么。第二层沿七个维度记录压力的程度和来源。第三层把这些判断与可观察的词语、句式和话语模式联系起来。第一层提供策略清单；后两层使不同策略能够相互比较，并支持超越二分类标签的错误分析。

Strategy Taxonomy
策略分类体系

Table [tab:taxonomy] presents the taxonomy. The four families organize the current negative-force scope by the dominant mechanism. Family A uses open requests, advice, orders, and reasoning. Family B motivates through an expected benefit or harm. Family C is social-normative: it asks the listener to act because of a duty, role, moral rule, authority, past favor, or group norm. Family D uses less direct tactics, including emotional exploitation, deception, false dilemmas, and hostility. These families are descriptive organizing units, not mutually exclusive psychological causes, and one dialogue may receive multiple type labels. The final column in Table [tab:taxonomy] records the dominant mechanism used by each type; these mechanisms are descriptive and do not imply mutually exclusive causes.

表 [tab:taxonomy] 展示了策略分类体系。四个大类按照主导机制组织当前负面能动力研究范围内的策略。A 类使用明确的请求、建议、命令和说理。B 类通过预期收益或伤害来激励行为。C 类属于社会规范类：它要求听者因为义务、角色、道德规则、权威、过去受过的帮助或群体规范而行动。D 类使用较间接的手段，包括情绪利用、欺骗、虚假两难和敌意。这些大类是用于描述和组织的类别，并不代表相互排斥的心理成因；同一段对话可以获得多个类型标签。表 [tab:taxonomy] 最后一列记录每种类型采用的主导机制；这些机制是描述性的，并不意味着各类成因相互排斥。

Measurement Dimensions
测量维度

Table [tab:dims] defines seven scores on a continuous 0–1 scale and gives each score a four-level label. Scores are assigned at both turn and dialogue level. The scores describe different parts of the same pressure. They are a measurement design for this benchmark, not a complete list of psychological traits. Their value depends on agreement, discrimination, and transfer.

表 [tab:dims] 在话轮级和对话级定义了七个维度，采用0–1连续量表，并配套四级离散标签。在当前负面能动力的研究范围内，列出的每一种策略至少对应一个维度。因此，这七个维度是本文提出的测量基础，并不声称在心理学意义上完备；它们是否有用，需要依据可靠性、区分价值和迁移能力来判断。这些维度直接依据分类体系中的机制定义，因此每段对话都能映射到同一个七维空间，并用同一套规则评估。

The dimensions answer different questions. D_1 measures how strongly the speaker pushes for an action. D_2 measures how much the listener's choice space is narrowed. A direct request can have high D_1 but low D_2 if the listener still has several clear alternatives. An ultimatum can have high D_2 because it presents few acceptable alternatives. D_3, D_4, and D_6 identify different pressure channels: social norms, emotional leverage, and overt hostility. A message may use one channel without using the others.

这些维度回答同一段对话中的不同问题。D_1 衡量说话者推动某项行动的力度，D_2 则衡量听者选择空间被缩小的程度。这两个分数可能相关，但不能互相替代：直接请求可以很强硬，却不排除其他选项；最后通牒即使用语简短，也可能大幅限制其他选项。D_3、D_4 和 D_6 区分不同的压力来源，即社会规范、情绪利用和明显敌意。一条信息可能只使用其中一种渠道，而不使用其他渠道。

D_5 measures factual or memory distortion. For example, “I never said that; you misremember” can be deceptive even when it is polite. An insult can be hostile without being deceptive, so D_5 and D_6 are separate. D_7 measures how directly the pressure is expressed. “You must stay” is more explicit than “A good daughter would stay”, but explicitness alone does not determine pressure strength. Together, the seven scores describe the push, the effect on available choices, the pressure channel, and the form of the expression.

D_5 刻画认知层面的扭曲，例如否认事件或编造事实，因此它不同于敌意：欺骗性语言可以很礼貌，而辱骂不一定包含虚假陈述。D_7 记录压力表达的直接程度。它是表达形式维度，而不是施压渠道，因此显性程度高本身并不意味着压力更强。将 D_7 单独保留，可以比较同一机制的直接和间接表达形式。这七个维度共同描述推动力度、对可选行动的影响、主要施压渠道和压力的表达形式；它们是测量轴，并不声称对应相互独立的心理特质。

The data also support simple checks of the dimension design. A moral-pressure dialogue should usually have high D_3, while a polite indirect appeal may have lower D_7. Family-level profiles and clustering test whether different strategy families concentrate on different dimensions.

已标注的对话也提供了验证集：具有道德胁迫的对话应获得较高的 D_3 和低至中等的 D_7 分数。我们还预期每个大类主要体现为少数维度上的高分，例如 C 类的 D_3、B 类的 D_2/D_4，以及 D 类的 D_5/D_6；我们通过各大类画像和聚类分析（第 [sec:dataset] 节）检查这些预期，将其作为质量检查和描述性证据。

标注示例

Table [tab:example] shows a worked annotation. The dialogue mixes moral appeal (C1) and obligation (C2), hence receives a multi-label type annotation; intensity is high and the profile is dominated by D_3 with secondary D_1. The example illustrates why the task is hard: the language is polite (no D_6) and the pressure implicit (low D_7)—the pattern that defeats lexical toxicity classifiers.

表 [tab:example] 给出了一个标注实例。该对话同时包含道德诉求（C1）和义务（C2），因此获得多标签类型标注；其强度较高，画像以 D_3 为主、D_1 为辅。这个例子说明了任务的难点：语言是礼貌的（无 D_6），压力却是隐含的（低 D_7），这类模式会使基于词汇的有毒语言分类器失效。

Two short examples show why context matters. In a normal request, one speaker says, “Could you close the window?” and the other replies, “Sure.” This is an A1 request with little pressure. The same request can carry more pressure when it follows “I helped you last time,” because the added context invokes C6 reciprocity. The wording alone is almost unchanged, but the dialogue-level profile changes because the listener's decision is framed as a debt.

两个简短例子说明了上下文为何重要。在普通请求中，一位说话者说“你能把窗户关上吗？”，另一位回答“当然可以”。这是一个压力很小的 A1 类请求。如果同样的请求出现在“上次我帮过你”之后，它就可能带来更大的压力，因为新增上下文引入了 C6 类互惠。措辞本身几乎没有变化，但由于听者的决定被置于“欠人情”的框架中，对话级画像发生了改变。

Dimension Structure and Interdependence
维度结构与相互依赖

The dimensions are not assumed independent; their correlation structure is an empirical object with explicit, falsifiable expectations. (i) D_1 should correlate with D_2 (the harder the push, the narrower the options), with transparent A1 as the predicted exception. (ii) D_3 and D_4 should co-occur in family C (guilt and moral appeal are emotionally loaded) but separate in family B (threats are affectively cold). (iii) D_5 should be roughly orthogonal to the others—gaslighting occurs in both polite and hostile registers—so a strong correlation with D_6 or D_7 would indicate annotation bias. (iv) D_7 should correlate negatively with D_5: covert strategies are typically indirect. The statistical analysis (Section [sec:dataset]) tests these via correlation matrices and per-family profiles; deviations are reported and used to revise the guideline rather than discarded.

本文不假设各维度相互独立；它们之间的相关结构是一个需要经验检验的问题，我们明确提出预期，以便加以证伪。（i）D_1 应与 D_2 相关，即推动越强，选项越少，但预期意图透明的 A1 是例外。（ii）D_3 和 D_4 应在 C 类中共同出现，因为内疚和道德诉求带有情绪色彩；但在 B 类中应有所分离，因为威胁在情感上是冷峻的。（iii）D_5 应与其他维度大致正交，因为煤气灯操纵既可采用礼貌语体，也可采用敌对语体；因此，它与 D_6 或 D_7 的强相关会表明存在标注偏差。（iv）D_7 应与 D_5 负相关，因为隐蔽策略通常是间接的。统计分析（第 [sec:dataset] 节）通过相关矩阵和各大类画像检验这些预期；偏离预期的结果会被报告并用于修订指南，而不是被丢弃。

Dataset Construction and Analysis
数据集构建与分析

Design Principles
设计原则

The dataset must cover the intended negative-force scope, provide comparable labels for every dialogue, and include hard negatives that separate pressure from mere toxicity or ordinary requests. Four principles guide construction. (i) De-identified multi-turn dialogues: conversational interaction with the turn structure and pragmatic context needed for dialogue-level analysis. (ii) Scope coverage: negative-force and non-force dialogue across the 0–5 range, so benign controls prevent degenerate models that classify any request as manipulation. (iii) Comparable measurement: every instance receives the same seven-dimension annotation on one scale. (iv) Hard negatives: polite pressure, sarcastic requests, and superficially kind manipulation stress-test lexical shortcuts.

数据集必须覆盖预定的负面能动力范围，为每段对话提供可比较的标签，并纳入能够区分施压、单纯有毒语言和普通请求的困难负例。数据构建遵循四项原则。（i）去标识化多轮对话：保留进行对话级分析所需的话轮结构和语用语境。（ii）范围覆盖：覆盖0–5范围内有负面能动力与无能动力的对话，通过无害对照避免模型退化为把所有请求都判为操纵。（iii）可比较的测量：每个样本都使用同一量表接受相同的七维标注。（iv）困难负例：礼貌施压、讽刺性请求和表面友善的操纵，用于检验模型是否依赖词汇捷径。

Sources and Sampling
来源与抽样

The release contains 4,066 de-identified, everyday multi-turn dialogues. We use 3,432 dialogues as the training split and 634 disjoint dialogues as the held-out split (Section [sec:firstrelease]). Each dialogue has binary pressure and 0–5 intensity annotations, which are used to evaluate the seven-dimension profiles. The release contributes new annotations, a unified taxonomy, and an evaluation protocol. The dialogues are de-identified, and provenance and licensing information are documented with the release.

数据集共包含4,066条去标识化的日常多轮对话，其中3,432条作为训练划分，634条作为互斥的留出划分（第 [sec:firstrelease] 节）。每段对话都有压力二分类标签和0–5强度标签，用于评估七维画像。本次发布的贡献是新的标注、统一的分类体系和评估方案。对话已经去标识化，数据来源和许可信息在发布材料中说明。

Annotation Protocol and Negative Cases
标注方案

The annotation follows the decision tree in Figure [fig:tree]. First, the annotator checks whether the dialogue targets a listener action or decision. If it does not, the dialogue is a negative example. Second, the annotator assigns all applicable strategy types. For example, one dialogue may contain both a moral appeal and social proof. Third, the annotator assigns the 0–5 intensity and the seven dimension scores. Each dimension receives a continuous 0–1 score and a four-level label: None, Low, Moderate, or High.

标注方案用于检验抽象定义是否可以一致地应用：先识别针对听者的行为目标，再识别机制，最后确定强度和维度画像。标注遵循三级决策树：（1）话语是否针对听者的某项行动或决定？如果否，则为负例。（2）出现了表 [tab:taxonomy] 中的哪些策略？这是多标签判断，例如一段对话可以同时包含道德诉求和社会认同。（3）强度和七个维度分别是多少？强度采用0–5的有序等级；每个维度提供0–1连续得分及四级离散标签（无／低／中／高）。本次发布中的标注由经过指令微调的大语言模型（第 [sec:exp] 节）生成；它遵循该决策树，在温度为0时为每段对话输出结构化 JSON 画像，以便复现。可靠性通过两种方式验证：与第 [sec:firstrelease] 节的二分类和强度标注进行一致性检查，以及在分层子样本上开展双人标注一致性研究（第 [sec:qc] 节）。

Negative examples use the same dimension scale as positive examples. Typical negative examples include pure information exchange, emotional venting with no behavioral target, and information-seeking questions. Advice that also acts as a subtle request receives every applicable type, while the dimension scores describe its dominant pressure channel. The released annotations are produced by an instruction-tuned LLM at temperature 0 using this protocol.

Quality Control and Consistency Validation
质量控制与一致性验证

Quality control combines three checks: agreement between independent annotators, consistency with the binary pressure and intensity labels, and the expected monotonicity of predicted intensity. For each dimension, the analysis reports Spearman correlations with the binary and 0–5 labels and its AUC for separating coercive from non-coercive dialogues (Table [tab:full]). It also checks whether predicted intensity increases with the gold label and whether the inter-dimension correlation matrix contains pathological coupling (Figures [fig:mono-full] and [fig:dim-corr-full]). These checks test whether the dimensions provide reproducible structure beyond a single binary judgment.

质量控制包含三项检查：独立标注者之间的一致性、与压力二分类和强度标签的一致性，以及预测强度是否按预期单调变化。对于每个维度，报告其与二分类标签和0–5强度标签的 Spearman 相关，以及区分施压与非施压对话的 AUC（表 [tab:full]）。同时检查预测强度是否随金标准标签增加，以及维度相关矩阵是否存在异常耦合（图 [fig:mono-full] 和图 [fig:dim-corr-full]）。这些检查用于判断七个维度是否提供了超出单一二分类判断的可复现结构。

Two graduate students in Computer Technology, including the first author, independently annotated 150 dialogues using a shared annotation rubric. Both annotators were proficient in reading English dialogues. Neither annotator had access to the model outputs during annotation. The sample was drawn from the 3,432-dialogue set by randomly selecting 75 dialogues from each binary-label class without replacement, followed by shuffling (random seed 2026). This balanced design covers both classes; the reported agreement therefore describes this sample rather than an unweighted estimate for the full dataset.

两名计算机技术专业研究生，包括第一作者，按照共同标注规则独立标注了150条对话。两名标注者都能熟练阅读英文对话，标注时看不到模型输出。样本从3,432条对话中抽取：在两个二分类类别中各随机无放回抽取75条，随后打乱顺序（随机种子为2026）。这种平衡抽样覆盖了两个类别，因此这里报告的一致性反映该样本，而不是完整数据集上的未加权估计。

Table [tab:iaa] reports inter-annotator agreement on the discrete labels. Intensity reaches substantial agreement (quadratic-weighted κ=0.823), as do normative pressure (0.830), toxicity (0.820), option constraint (0.776), directive force (0.771), and emotional pressure (0.758). Explicitness reaches moderate agreement (0.543). These estimates concern discrete labels and do not directly establish the reliability of the continuous model scores.

表 [tab:iaa] 报告了离散标签上的标注者间一致性。强度达到较高一致性（二次加权 κ=0.823），规范压力（0.830）、有毒性（0.820）、选项约束（0.776）、指令力度（0.771）和情绪压力（0.758）也达到这一水平。显性程度达到中等一致性（0.543）。这些结果针对的是离散标签，不能直接证明模型连续分数的可靠性。

统计分析

The analysis compares each score with the binary and 0–5 labels, inspects inter-dimension correlations, compare profiles for coercive and non-coercive dialogues, and test whether predicted intensity follows the ordered labels. Type-level results are reported in Section [sec:results].

统计分析检验七个维度是否刻画了压力的不同部分。我们将每个分数与二分类和0–5标签比较，检查维度间相关性，比较施压与非施压对话的画像，并检验预测强度是否符合有序标签。类型级结果见第 [sec:results] 节。

Consistency Check on the Held-Out Set
留出集上的一致性检查

The held-out analysis asks whether the seven-dimensional profile follows the pressure and intensity labels before the training split is described. Agreement is a necessary check, but it does not show that the dimensions cause changes in listener behavior. The held-out split contains 634 dialogues: 332 coercive (52.4%) and 302 non-coercive, with gold intensity distributed as 170/54/78/64/107/161 over levels 0–5.

本小节在描述训练划分之前，检验七维画像能否恢复压力和强度标签的顺序。与二分类和强度标注一致，是必要检查，但不能证明这些维度会导致听者行为变化。留出划分包含634条对话，其中332条施压（52.4%）、302条非施压；0–5各强度等级的标签数量依次为170/54/78/64/107/161。

Table [tab:rq1] reports the consistency of the extracted profiles with the binary and intensity annotations. All seven dimensions correlate with the binary pressure label and with the 0–5 intensity label; the strongest channels are option constraint (D_2), directive force (D_1), and emotional pressure (D_4), followed by explicitness (D_7) and normative pressure (D_3). Each dimension separates coercive from non-coercive dialogues with AUC between 0.72 and 0.83. The predicted intensity rises monotonically with the gold label (Spearman ρ=0.66), and the inferred binary decision reaches AUC 0.83 and F1 0.80 at the fixed intensity threshold of 3. The training-split descriptive statistics are reported in Section [sec:fullscale].

表 [tab:rq1] 报告了提取画像与二分类及强度标注的一致性。七个维度都与压力二分类标签和0–5强度标签相关；相关性较强的维度包括选项约束（D_2）、指令力度（D_1）和情绪压力（D_4），其次是显性程度（D_7）和规范压力（D_3）。每个维度区分施压与非施压对话的 AUC 都在0.72到0.83之间。预测强度随标签单调上升（Spearman ρ=0.66），在固定强度阈值3下，推导出的二分类判断达到 AUC 0.83、F1 0.80。训练划分的描述性统计见第 [sec:fullscale] 节。

本小节描述完整发布集的标签分布和维度画像。这里是描述性分析，不是独立测试集，因为完整发布集也用于后续实验。数据集包含3,432条对话，其中1,862条（54.2%）施压，1,570条（45.8%）不施压。金标准强度在0–5级上的数量分布为986/267/317/337/621/904。0级困难负例占28.7%，4–5级高强度对话占44.4%。

Experimental Design and Evaluation Protocol
实验设计与评估方案

The experiments test whether the agentive-force profile supports prediction beyond description. T1 measures pressure detection, T2 strategy recognition, and T3 intensity prediction. Ablation and cross-domain tests measure the role of individual dimensions and transfer to other resources.

实验检验能动力画像是否支持预测，而不只是用于描述。T1 衡量压力检测，T2 衡量策略识别，T3 衡量强度预测。消融实验和跨领域实验分别检验各维度的作用，以及画像向其他资源迁移的能力。

Tasks
任务

LINGUAFORCE defines three tasks at increasing granularity. T1 (agentive-force detection) is a binary classification of whether a dialogue exerts negative pressure beyond ordinary non-coercive controls. T2 (type recognition) is a fifteen-way multi-label classification of the strategies in Table [tab:taxonomy]; type labels are released for both the held-out split and the training split. T3 (intensity prediction) is ordinal regression of the 0–5 intensity, evaluated with quadratic weighted kappa (QWK) and accuracy-within-±1.

LINGUAFORCE 定义了三个粒度逐步提高的任务。T1（能动力检测）是二分类任务，判断对话是否施加了超出普通非施压对照的负面压力。T2（类型识别）是十五类多标签分类，类别见表 [tab:taxonomy]；留出集和完整发布集都提供类型标签。T3（强度预测）是对0–5强度进行有序回归，使用二次加权 κ（QWK）和误差不超过±1的准确率评估。

Two-Stage Pipeline
两阶段流程

The two-stage pipeline tests whether the explicit profile adds useful structure beyond a direct judgment. An upstream LLM converts each dialogue into turn-level and dialogue-level dimension scores, and a downstream model uses them for T1–T3. For comparison, a direct judge predicts the label from the dialogue without producing the seven-dimension profile. The experiments compare three ways to combine information: turn-only, dialogue-only, and turn-plus- dialogue representations.

两阶段流程检验显式画像是否能在直接判断之外提供有用结构。上游大语言模型把每段对话转换为话轮级和对话级的七维分数，下游模型利用这些分数完成 T1–T3。作为对照，直接判断器不生成七维画像，而是直接根据对话预测标签。实验还比较三种信息组合方式：只使用话轮信息、只使用整段对话信息，以及结合两者。

The pipeline is evaluated zero-shot: a frozen instruction-tuned LLM parses each dialogue into the profile, and a direct judge that skips the profile serves as control. The main results use DeepSeek. A second frozen parser, GPT-6 Astra, is evaluated on the held-out consistency set to test parser sensitivity. Prompt variants include zero-shot, chain-of-thought [wei2022], and self-reflection, following the self-perception approach of MultiManip [multimanip] (Table [tab:models]).

该流程采用零样本评估：冻结参数的指令微调大语言模型将每段对话解析为画像，不经过画像的直接判断器作为对照。主要结果使用 DeepSeek。第二个冻结参数的解析器 GPT-6 Astra 在留出一致性检查集上接受评估，用于测试对解析器选择的敏感性。提示词变体包括零样本、思维链 [wei2022] 和自我反思，并参考 MultiManip 的自我感知方法 [multimanip]（表 [tab:models]）。

Experimental Setup
实验设置

All parsers run at temperature 0. T1 reports binary AUROC and F1 at the fixed intensity threshold of 3; T3 uses Spearman correlation, QWK, and accuracy within ±1. T2 reports macro- and micro-F1; its exploratory best-threshold values use the candidate thresholds described in Table [tab:rq2]. Cross-domain transfer uses the same frozen parser on four public corpora.

所有解析器都在温度0下运行。T1 报告二分类 AUC 和最佳 F1；T3 报告 Spearman 相关、QWK 以及误差不超过±1的准确率。跨领域迁移使用同一个冻结解析器处理四个公开语料库。

Parser sensitivity is evaluated on the held-out set with the same dimensions, prompt format, temperature, and output schema; the corresponding results are reported with the main results.

解析器敏感性在留出集上评估，使用相同的维度、提示词格式、温度和输出结构；对应结果与主要结果一起报告。

Research Questions
研究问题

The experiments examine four questions: whether the dimensions agree with the pressure annotations, whether they provide complementary information, whether they transfer to related forms of pressure, and whether the results are robust to implementation choices. RQ1 (annotation consistency): Do the seven dimensions align with both the binary pressure and 0–5 intensity annotations? The evaluation reports each dimension's Spearman correlation with these labels and its AUC for separating coercive from non-coercive dialogues. Results are reported separately for the disjoint held-out split (n=634) and the training split (n=3,432) in Sections [sec:firstrelease]–[sec:fullscale].

实验围绕四个问题展开：七个维度是否与压力标签一致，是否提供互补信息，能否迁移到相关的压力形式，以及结果是否对实现方式保持稳健。RQ1（标注一致性）：七个维度是否同时与压力二分类和0–5强度标签一致？我们报告每个维度与这两类标签的 Spearman 相关，以及区分施压与非施压对话的 AUC。结果分别在互相独立的634条留出评估集和3,432条训练发布集上报告，见第 [sec:firstrelease]–[sec:fullscale] 节。

RQ2 (dimension structure): Do the seven dimensions carry distinct, complementary signal? Report per-dimension scores and AUCs, examine the inter-dimension correlation matrix for expected couplings (e.g., D_3–D_4) and unexpected ones (e.g., D_5–D_6), and analyze per-family profiles (Section [sec:results]).

RQ2（维度结构）：七个维度是否携带不同且互补的信号？报告各维度得分和 AUC，检查维度间相关矩阵中的预期关联（例如 D_3–D_4）和非预期关联（例如 D_5–D_6），并分析各大类画像（第 [sec:results] 节）。

RQ3 (zero-shot generalization): Can the profile detect pressure in strategies that are absent from the training data? We apply the frozen pipeline to external corpora and evaluate leave-one-type-out transfer within the LINGUAFORCE taxonomy. The results are reported in the main results and cross-domain sections.

RQ3（零样本泛化）：七维画像能否检测训练数据中没有出现的策略所带来的压力？我们将冻结流程应用到外部语料，并在 LINGUAFORCE 分类体系内进行留一类型迁移实验。结果在主要结果和跨领域实验部分报告。

RQ4 (implementation choices): How do aggregation and prompt design affect the pipeline? We compare global-only, turn-mean, and combined aggregation with the same linear readout, and evaluate zero-shot, chain-of-thought, and self-reflection prompts. The results are reported in the main results and Tables [tab:agg]–[tab:prompt].

RQ4（实现方式）：聚合方式和提示词设计如何影响流程？使用相同的线性读出模型比较只使用整段对话、话轮均值和二者结合的聚合方式，并评估零样本、思维链和自我反思提示词。结果见主要结果以及表 [tab:agg]–[tab:prompt]。

Cross-Domain Evaluation
跨领域评估

The cross-domain test asks whether the profile transfers to related listener-directed pressure while remaining distinct from toxicity without a behavioral target. The zero-shot pipeline is applied to MentalManip and MultiManip (psychological manipulation), TalkDown (patronizing language), and ToxicChat (toxicity). Each corpus is evaluated with its standard metric and threshold-free AUROC.

跨领域实验检验画像能否迁移到相关的面向听者的压力，同时与没有行为目标的有毒语言区分开。零样本流程应用于 MentalManip 和 MultiManip（心理操纵）、TalkDown（居高临下语言）以及 ToxicChat（有毒语言），每个语料库都报告其标准指标和无阈值 AUROC。

Dimension Contribution and Ablation
维度作用与消融分析

This analysis asks whether the seven dimensions provide complementary information. Three checks are used. First, each dimension is compared with the gold pressure and intensity labels. Second, a simple linear model uses the seven scores to predict the labels. Third, each dimension is removed in turn to measure its marginal contribution.

本分析检验七个维度是否提供互补信息。采用三项检查。第一，将每个维度与金标准压力和强度标签比较。第二，用一个简单的线性模型根据七个分数预测标签。第三，每次去掉一个维度，测量它的边际作用。线性模型在训练发布集上训练、在留出集上评估，二分类 AUC 为0.855，强度 Spearman 为0.674。它略高于大语言模型的维度条件读出结果（AUC 0.831，Spearman 0.665），说明七个分数在不使用另一个大语言模型的情况下仍保留了与决策有关的信号。表 [tab:ablation] 的逐维度消融结果显示，不同维度承担的作用不同。去掉有毒性（D_6）对压力检测影响最大，AUC 下降0.008；去掉规范压力（D_3）对强度预测影响最大，Spearman 下降0.021。虽然 D_6 单独与标签的相关性最弱，但它有助于区分有敌意、却没有行为目标的对话。D_4 与 D_3 的信息重叠较多，其余维度的边际影响较小。结果支持组合使用多个维度，而不是依赖某一个决定性维度。

The linear model, trained on the training split and evaluated on the held-out split, reaches binary AUC 0.855 and intensity Spearman 0.674. These results are close to, and slightly higher than, the profile-based LLM pipeline (AUC 0.831, Spearman 0.665). Both systems use the seven scores produced by the parser; the comparison therefore shows that a simple downstream model can recover the main predictive signal from the profile. It does not show that the linear model replaces the upstream LLM. The leave-one-dimension-out results in Table [tab:ablation] show different roles for different dimensions. Removing toxicity (D_6) hurts pressure detection most (AUC drop 0.008), while removing normative pressure (D_3) hurts intensity prediction most (Spearman drop 0.021). Although D_6 has the weakest individual correlation, it helps separate hostile dialogues that do not contain a behavioral target. D_4 overlaps substantially with D_3; removing D_4 slightly improves both metrics, which suggests redundancy or noise in this readout. The remaining dimensions have small marginal effects. These changes should be read as marginal effects, not as evidence that any single dimension is indispensable. Overall, the results support a combined representation rather than reliance on one decisive dimension.

线性模型在训练发布集上训练、在留出集上评估，二分类 AUC 为0.855，强度 Spearman 为0.674。这个结果与基于七维画像的大语言模型流程相近，并略高于后者（AUC 0.831，Spearman 0.665）。两种方法都使用解析器生成的七个分数；这个比较说明，在得到七维画像后，简单的下游模型也能读出其中主要的预测信号，但不能说明线性模型可以替代上游大语言模型。表 [tab:ablation] 的逐维度消融结果显示，不同维度承担的作用不同。去掉有毒性（D_6）对压力检测影响最大，AUC 下降0.008；去掉规范压力（D_3）对强度预测影响最大，Spearman 下降0.021。虽然 D_6 单独与标签的相关性最弱，但它有助于区分有敌意、却没有行为目标的对话。D_4 与 D_3 的信息重叠较多；去掉 D_4 后两个指标反而略有提升，说明在这个读出模型中存在一定的信息冗余或噪声。其余维度的边际影响较小。这些变化应理解为维度的边际作用，不能据此认为某一个维度不可替代。总体而言，结果支持组合使用多个维度，而不是依赖某一个决定性维度。

Dimension Differences by Strategy Family
不同策略大类的维度差异

This section compares the seven-dimensional profiles of the strategy families. The goal is to see whether different families show different patterns across the dimensions, while allowing for overlap between related strategies. Figure [fig:tsne] uses t-SNE only for visualization: it maps the 3,432 training-split profiles to two dimensions and colors them by dominant family, with the 226 dialogues containing no detected strategy shown as benign. The families show some separation in this view, while benign dialogues tend to lie on the low-pressure side. To quantify family-level structure, we train a multinomial logistic classifier on the seven dimensions to predict the four strategy families plus the benign group. Five-fold cross-validation gives an accuracy of 0.640, compared with 0.200 chance accuracy. The profiles therefore contain family-level signal, although the moderate accuracy indicates substantial overlap between families. Each dimension alone separates coercive from non-coercive dialogues with AUC 0.71–0.83, supporting pressure detection but not family-specific separation. Figure [fig:famheat] reports each family's mean dimension profile: the social-normative family peaks on normative and emotional pressure (D_3, D_4), the exchange family on option constraint and toxicity (D_2, D_6), the covert family on emotional pressure (D_4), and benign dialogues are uniformly low across all dimensions.

本节比较不同策略大类的七维画像，考察不同大类是否在这些维度上呈现不同模式，同时允许相关策略之间存在重叠。图 [fig:tsne] 仅将 t-SNE 用于可视化：它把3,432条训练划分对话画像映射到二维，并按主导策略大类着色；其中还标出了226条未检测到策略的无害对话。从图中可以看到，不同大类有一定分离趋势，无害对话则通常位于低压力一侧。为定量检验策略大类结构，我们使用七个维度训练一个多项逻辑回归分类器，预测四个策略大类或无害组。五折交叉验证的准确率为0.640，五分类随机准确率为0.200。这说明七维画像包含家族级信号，但中等的准确率也表明不同大类之间存在明显重叠。单独使用任一维度区分施压和非施压对话时，AUC 为0.71–0.83，说明这些维度有助于压力检测，但不能区分具体策略大类。图 [fig:famheat] 展示各大类的平均维度画像：社会规范类在规范压力和情绪压力（D_3、D_4）上最高，交换类在选项约束和有毒性（D_2、D_6）上最高，隐蔽类主要集中在情绪压力（D_4），无害对话在所有维度上的分数都较低。

Main Results
主要结果

Results are reported for pressure detection and intensity prediction, cross-domain transfer, strategy recognition, and implementation choices.

本节依次报告压力检测与强度预测、跨领域迁移、策略识别和实现方式的结果。

Pressure detection and intensity. Table [tab:results-t1] and Table [tab:results-t23] report the dimension-conditioned pipeline on the 3,432-dialogue training split and the disjoint 634-dialogue held-out split. The pipeline first maps each dialogue to a seven-dimension profile (Section [sec:exp]) and then reads out the binary pressure label and the 0–5 intensity. On the training split, it reaches a binary AUC of 0.852 and an F1 of 0.817 at threshold 3; the corresponding held-out values are 0.831 and 0.795. Intensity prediction reaches Spearman ρ=0.651, QWK 0.595, and accuracy within ±1 of 0.692 on the training split, compared with ρ=0.665 and QWK 0.623 on the held-out split. On the held-out split, a direct judge that skips the profile reaches AUC 0.780 and F1 0.755 at threshold 3. The held-out parser comparison gives AUC 0.831 for DeepSeek and 0.838 for GPT-6 Astra, with intensity Spearman 0.665 and 0.629, respectively. Binary detection is similar, while intensity ranking differs between parsers.

压力检测与强度预测。表 [tab:results-t1] 和表 [tab:results-t23] 报告了维度条件流程在3,432条训练划分对话和634条互斥留出划分对话上的结果。流程先把每段对话映射为七维画像（第 [sec:exp] 节），再读出压力二分类标签和0–5强度。在训练划分上，二分类 AUC 为0.852，固定阈值3下的 F1 为0.817；留出划分上分别为0.831和0.795。强度预测在训练划分上的 Spearman 为0.651，QWK 为0.595，误差不超过±1的准确率为0.692；留出划分上的 Spearman 为0.665，QWK 为0.623。留出划分上，跳过七维画像的直接判断器在阈值3下达到 AUC 0.780、F1 0.755。解析器比较中，DeepSeek 与 GPT-6 Astra 在留出划分上的 AUC 分别为0.831和0.838，强度 Spearman 分别为0.665和0.629。两者的二分类检测接近，但强度排序存在差异。

Cross-domain transfer. The cross-domain results in Table [tab:results-x] evaluate the frozen pipeline on four external corpora. Threshold-free AUROC is 0.742 on MentalManip, 0.706 on MultiManip, and 0.729 on TalkDown, indicating moderate transfer to psychological manipulation and patronizing language. Transfer to ToxicChat is weaker (AUROC 0.587), consistent with generic toxicity being less tied to a listener-directed behavioral target. At the fixed source threshold of 3, F1 is much lower on TalkDown (0.058), so ranking performance does not imply that the same decision threshold transfers. The table therefore reports AUROC as the primary cross-domain metric and fixed-threshold scores only as descriptive reference values.

跨领域迁移。表 [tab:results-x] 报告冻结流程在四个外部语料上的表现。MentalManip、MultiManip 和 TalkDown 的无阈值 AUROC 分别为0.742、0.706和0.729，表明对心理操纵和居高临下表达有中等程度的迁移；ToxicChat 上较弱（AUROC 0.587），与一般有毒语言较少直接指向听者行为的解释相符。在来源任务固定阈值3下，TalkDown 的 F1 仅为0.058，说明排序表现不能保证相同决策阈值也适用于外部语料。因此，表格以 AUROC 作为主要跨领域指标，将固定阈值结果仅作为描述性参考。

Strategy recognition. Table [tab:rq2] compares a one-vs-rest logistic readout over the seven dimensions with a supervised RoBERTa-base classifier. Training on the 3,432-dialogue training split and evaluating on the held-out split gives the dimension readout family-level macro/micro-F1 of 0.772/0.827 and type-level macro/micro-F1 of 0.480/0.668. Five-fold cross-validation on the training split gives similar values (family 0.752/0.820; type 0.477/0.669). RoBERTa scores 0.780/0.820 at the family level and 0.544/0.731 at the type level on the held-out split. The dimension readout is competitive at the family level, while RoBERTa performs better on individual types. Three types have fewer than 30 training examples: rational persuasion (A_3), authority (C_4), and deception/gaslighting (D_2); their scarcity warrants caution when interpreting type-level macro-F1. The reported F1 thresholds were selected on the respective evaluation sets, so these are exploratory best-threshold scores rather than performance at a prespecified threshold.

策略识别。表 [tab:rq2] 比较了基于七维分数的一对其余逻辑回归读出模型与有监督的 RoBERTa-base 分类器。在3,432条训练划分上训练、634条留出划分上评估，维度读出模型的大类识别 macro/micro-F1 为0.772/0.827，类型识别为0.480/0.668。训练划分上的五折交叉验证结果相近：大类为0.752/0.820，类型为0.477/0.669。RoBERTa 在留出划分上的大类结果为0.780/0.820，类型结果为0.544/0.731。维度读出模型在大类级表现相近，RoBERTa 在具体类型上更强。理性说服（A_3）、权威（C_4）和欺骗／煤气灯操纵（D_2）在训练集中各不足30条，因此解释类型级 macro-F1 时需要谨慎。这里的 F1 阈值是在各自评估集上选出的，因此这些是探索性的最佳阈值结果，不是预先固定阈值下的性能。

The leave-one-type-out analysis yields mean novelty AUROC 0.54, close to chance. Separately, predicted intensity distinguishes dialogues with any annotated strategy from those with none (AUROC 0.963). This latter test includes known strategies and does not measure detection of held-out types on its own.

留一类型分析的平均新颖性 AUROC 为0.54，接近随机水平。另一个实验中，预测强度区分“含任意已标注策略”和“无策略”的对话，AUROC 为0.963。后者包含已知策略，不能单独衡量对留出类型的检测能力。

Implementation choices. The implementation ablations in Tables [tab:agg]–[tab:prompt] show that turn-only mean pooling is near chance (AUC 0.525), whereas combining turn-level and dialogue-level profiles gives the best result (AUC 0.859, Spearman 0.679, QWK 0.680). The three prompt variants yield similar results (AUC 0.83–0.84), indicating limited sensitivity to prompt wording.

实现方式。表 [tab:agg]–[tab:prompt] 的消融结果显示，只对话轮分数做均值池化时 AUC 接近随机水平（0.525）；结合话轮级和对话级画像得到最高结果（AUC 0.859、Spearman 0.679、QWK 0.680）。三种提示词的 AUC 接近（0.83–0.84），说明在所测的提示词变体之间差异较小。

Limitations and Threats to Validity
局限性与有效性风险

The evidence has four boundaries. First, the seven dimensions are a measurement design rather than a complete theory of influence, and the 150-dialogue agreement study is a limited reliability check. Second, the release contains English dialogues from sampled everyday scenarios; transfer results on four external corpora do not establish cross-cultural or cross-lingual validity. Third, parser comparisons cover only two models, and F1 values whose thresholds are selected on the evaluation set are exploratory rather than fixed-threshold estimates. Finally, the labels measure pressure expressed in dialogue, not whether a listener complied or was harmed.

这些证据有四个边界。第一，七个维度是一套测量设计，而不是完整的影响理论；150条对话的一致性研究也只是有限的可靠性检查。第二，数据集由抽样得到的英文日常对话组成，在四个外部语料上的迁移结果不能证明跨文化或跨语言有效。第三，解析器比较只覆盖两个模型，且在评估集上选择阈值得到的 F1 属于探索性结果，不代表固定阈值下的性能。最后，标签衡量的是对话中表达的压力，不代表听者是否服从或受到伤害。

Discussion
讨论

The experiments support a practical interpretation of the framework. A single profile makes heterogeneous negative strategies comparable, while the cross-domain results suggest that some of their signal transfers beyond the training taxonomy. The profile also makes predictions inspectable through dimension-level evidence. These findings support measurement and analysis; they do not imply that the dimensions are independent psychological traits or that the benchmark predicts individual responses.

这些实验支持对该框架的实践性理解。统一画像使不同的负面策略可以在同一空间中比较，跨领域结果则表明，其中一部分信号能够迁移到训练分类体系之外。维度级证据也使预测结果更容易检查。这些发现支持测量和分析，但不意味着这些维度是相互独立的心理特质，也不意味着基准能够预测个体反应。

Conclusion
结论

This paper presents LINGUAFORCE, a dataset and benchmark for measuring negative agentive force in multi-turn dialogue. The release provides intensity labels, seven dimension profiles, strategy labels, and an evaluation protocol. The experiments show that the profiles support pressure detection, intensity ordering, and transfer to related manipulation and patronizing-language corpora. The benchmark is a measurement and analysis resource, not a causal model of listener behavior. Future work should broaden the annotation study, evaluate more parsers, and test other languages and forms of influence.

本文提出 LINGUAFORCE，一个用于衡量多轮对话中负面话语能动力的数据集与基准。该发布版本提供强度标签、七维画像、策略标签和评估方案。实验表明，这些画像支持压力检测、强度排序，并能迁移到相关的心理操纵和居高临下语言语料。该基准是测量和分析资源，而不是听者行为的因果模型。后续工作应扩大标注研究、评估更多解析器，并测试其他语言和其他形式的影响。

Ethics and Privacy
伦理与隐私

The dialogues are de-identified English text. The release adds no personal information or new data collection. The study does not collect behavioral or demographic data; the manual checks use already-anonymized text. Annotators may skip distressing content and take breaks. Because the taxonomy could be misused to craft manipulative language, the release is intended for research on detection and analysis, not automated moderation, surveillance, or consequential decisions without human review. The current data are English and drawn from everyday scenarios, so cultural and cross-lingual validity remains an open question. A datasheet documents composition, preprocessing, intended uses, and limitations.

对话是去标识化的英文文本。本次发布不增加个人信息，也没有开展新的数据收集。本研究不收集行为或人口统计数据，人工检查使用的是已匿名化文本。标注者可以跳过令人不适的内容并休息。由于该分类体系可能被滥用于编写操纵性语言，发布内容仅用于操纵检测和分析；在没有人工审核的情况下，不应将其用于自动化审核、监控或重要决策。当前数据使用英语，来自日常场景，因此其文化适用性和跨语言有效性仍需进一步研究。数据说明表记录了数据构成、预处理、预期用途和局限性。
